# Doro Creator Toolkit

Create **static and animated carousels** you can review, edit and share. Tell your AI agent what you want to create; it handles setup and builds a draft. You stay in control of the words, visuals and final files.

Made by [DoroSocial](https://doro.social). Creating and downloading content does not require a Doro account.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Create your carousel. Let your agent schedule it with DoroSocial.**
>
> Go from a finished image carousel to scheduled posts across your connected, supported social platforms. Ask your agent to schedule it for later or publish it now through DoroSocial. You review and approve the accounts, slides, captions and timing; your connected agent handles the sending and scheduling.
>
> A Doro connection is needed for this step. Creating, editing and downloading remain available without one.

Choose still-image slides, animated video slides, or a mix of both. You can also download a complete still version as images or a LinkedIn PDF.

## Create your first carousel

**You do not need to run installation commands yourself.** Use an AI agent that can work with files and run software on your computer. A regular browser chat on its own cannot set up this local app.

1. Open your agent on your computer.
2. Paste the request below and change the topic and audience.
3. Let the agent set up the toolkit and create your draft. Open the preview link it gives you.

> Use https://github.com/Doro-Social/doro-creator-toolkit to create a carousel about choosing espresso or Americano for first-time café customers. Read its README and carousel-creator skill, handle installation and startup for me, and create a useful draft. Use a clearly fictional coffee shop for this example. Review every slide and fix any problems, then give me the preview link. Do not publish anything.

For your own business, tell the agent your topic, who the content is for and what you want them to learn. You can provide a logo, photos and brand colors too. If a necessary detail is missing, the agent should ask rather than invent it.

Your agent may ask you to approve installing software or accessing files. First-time setup needs an internet connection and can take a few minutes. Once the studio is open, you can also edit directly without an agent.

## What you can make today

- **Static carousels:** explain an idea, share a checklist or compare options with swipeable image slides.
- **LinkedIn documents:** download your slides as a PDF.
- **Animated carousels:** bring selected slides to life and export individual video clips alongside any still slides. A full-video export is also available.
- **Content in your branding:** use your own words, colors, photos and logo.

Start with a fictional coffee-shop template or ask your agent to create content for your subject. Portrait, square, story and landscape sizes are available.

The animation library shows creative examples. Some treatments are available in the editor; the gallery films are not all ready-to-apply effects. Your agent should choose motion that helps the content, and leave slides still when that works better.

## Review it, make it yours, download it

Your agent creates the draft. The studio is where you review it.

- Read the whole carousel and check that the information is right.
- Change the text, images, slide order or branding yourself, or ask your agent to revise it.
- Save your changes and download images, a PDF or video.

You can ask for changes in ordinary language:

> Make the opening more specific and shorten the explanation on slide three.

> Use my logo and colors, and make a square version without changing the original.

> Check every slide, then export images for Instagram and a PDF for LinkedIn.

Nothing is published when you create or download a carousel.

## Come back to it later

Save your logo, icons and photos once for each business. Ask your agent to use that brand’s images the next time you make a post.

Your projects are saved on your computer. Ask your agent:

> Open my existing Doro Creator Toolkit and help me continue the carousel I was working on. Keep my saved projects.

If a preview link stops working, ask the agent to reopen the toolkit. You do not need to reinstall it for each session. Use **Back up project** in the editor to keep a portable copy of work you want to preserve.

## Schedule or publish with DoroSocial

Ask your agent to take the finished carousel from creation to scheduled posts, without manually uploading it to each supported destination:

> Help me schedule this image carousel through DoroSocial. Show me my connected, supported accounts, adapt the caption for each destination, and confirm the posting times with me. Once I approve the plan, send the carousel and schedule the posts.

You can also ask to publish now. Your agent needs a Doro connection and must check which destinations support the selected content.

For a finished image carousel, choose **Prepare Doro handoff with my agent** after exporting. This copies instructions for your agent. An agent connected to Doro can then help you choose the account and review the exact slides and caption before sending. Copying the instructions does not upload or publish anything.

PDFs and videos remain available to download; sending those formats directly through this integration has not been verified.

## A few useful things to know

**Does this include an AI assistant?** No. You use your own agent alongside the toolkit. The toolkit does not require you to buy an AI subscription or enter an AI API key into the studio. Your chosen agent may have its own pricing and setup requirements.

**Where does my work go?** The toolkit stores projects and exports on your computer. Your agent has its own data-handling settings. Sending content to Doro is a separate, deliberate step.

**Is it ready for everyone?** This is an early working version. The local workflow has been tested on macOS, but testing on fresh computers and other operating systems is still incomplete. Animation quality and pacing are still being improved.
